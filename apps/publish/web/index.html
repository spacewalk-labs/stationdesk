How to use it

  1. Copy the request and paste it into Claude Code or Codex on this StationDesk.
  2. Tell your agent what to explain and who will read it.
  3. Open the link your agent returns. Find and manage the document here.

An internal link follows this StationDesk’s access settings. To share outside your tailnet, use the document’s Share menu and choose an access method and expiry.
